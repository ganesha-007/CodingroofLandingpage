# Industry photos — Higgsfield prompts

Generate one image per prompt (landscape **4:3**, photoreal model), then tell Claude.
Claude will pull them from your Higgsfield history and save them as
`src/assets/industries/<file>.jpg` — the Industries cards switch to photo cards automatically.

**Shared style (append to every prompt):**
> Editorial documentary photograph, candid and natural, soft daylight, clean modern interior,
> subtle cool blue-grey colour grade, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, realistic skin and
> textures, no text, no logos, no watermarks.
