## What 60 SaaS home pages taught us

Reviewed: Zoho, Zoho One, Odoo, Shopify, Stripe, Linear, monday.com, Notion, HubSpot, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Fresha, Vagaro, Glofox, Square, Vyapar, myBillBook, ServiceTitan, Zenoti, Gofrugal, Petpooja, LeadSquared, Keka, Razorpay, Pipedrive, Katana, Xero, Attio, Intercom, Ramp, Mercury, Rippling, Deel, FreshBooks, Basecamp, Close, Procore, Mindbody, Darwinbox, Chargebee, ClickUp, Asana, Airtable, Mangomint, Boulevard, Shopmonkey, Workiz, SimplePractice, BUSY, Calendly, Superhuman, Vercel, Loom, Retool, Brex, ServiceM8, Loandisk, Printavo, PostHog, Webflow. (Freshworks, Toast, Lightspeed, Gusto, NetSuite, Tally and Wodify blocked automated reading and are not counted.)

The patterns the strongest ones share:

1. **Hero headlines are 4 to 7 words** and promise an outcome, not a category. The best read like a slogan you could paint on a wall.
2. **Two-beat couplets win.** Short parallel lines with a twist ("X. Y."), used by Ramp, Attio, Zenoti, Close and others. They are memorable and scan in under a second.
3. **The subheadline does the explaining.** It names the concrete jobs (quote, invoice, stock, booking, payment) and the span ("from first quote to final payment"), so the headline can stay short.
4. **Vertical software names the customer's world**: shop life, the trades, the counter, the branch. Generic "businesses" is weaker than "your trade".
5. **They remove the fear of switching**: "switching is simple", "set up for you". For SMBs in India this is the biggest objection.
6. **Proof is specific numbers**, never adjectives (bill in 8 seconds, 30,000+ stores, 6,00,000+ businesses).
7. **Section headings are benefits, not labels.** "Turn new clients into regulars" beats "Marketing features".
8. **Problem-first lines create urgency**: "the lead that waits is the lead you lose", "why projects slip when work is scattered".
9. **One clear CTA**, usually "Book a demo" for vertical B2B, with the second CTA lower-commitment.

Rules kept: only real claims (approved stats: 3.4x ROI, 12+ products, 14+ countries, 6-week MVP; data migration and support by CodingRoof), no copied competitor lines, no em dashes.

## Hero (the part that matters most)

### Option A (recommended): couplet, keeps the brush stroke on "business"

> **Eyebrow:** 12 ready-to-run products · AI built in
>
> # Less paperwork. More ~~business~~.
> *(gold paint-brush stroke stays on "business")*
>
> Billing, stock, loans, bookings and sales, on software built for how your industry actually works. From the first lead to the final invoice, with AI doing the busywork.
>
> **[Book a free demo]**  **[See all 12 products]**
>
> We handle setup, data migration and support.

Why: the strongest pattern in the research (two-beat couplet), names the pain every SMB owner feels (paperwork), and ends on the word we already animate. The subhead does the explaining; the trust line removes the switching fear.

### Option B: industry fit

> # Software that knows your ~~business~~.
>
> Gold loan, retail, salon, textile, print and more. Ready-to-run software built around your industry's day, with AI that handles the busywork.

Why: it leads with the real differentiator (industry-specific products, not a generic suite). It's slightly softer than A.

### Option C: problem-first

> # Your ~~business~~ deserves better than spreadsheets.
>
> Move billing, stock, loans and bookings onto software built for your industry. We set it up and move your data, so you don't start from zero.

Why: maximum urgency for owners still on Excel, WhatsApp or notebooks. It's longer, and the tone is more confrontational.

### Option D: keep today's line, sharpen everything around it

> # The software your ~~business~~ runs on.
>
> (Option A's subhead, CTAs and trust line.)

Why: the safest choice. The headline already works; the gain comes from a sharper subhead and the switching-fear line.

**Note:** the browser tab / Google title stays "CodingRoof | Software your business runs on" with any option. That's our brand line, and changing it would reset what Google just started re-indexing.
